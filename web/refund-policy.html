Refund Policy

When you can get your money back, how to ask, and how long it takes.

Effective 29 August 2026 · Last updated 29 August 2026

01Summary

The short version. EU and UK consumers get a 14-day right to withdraw. Beyond that, we offer a 14-day goodwill refund on first-time subscription purchases, pro-rata refunds if we cause a material problem, and refunds of unused purchased Credits within 14 days. Credits you have already consumed cannot be refunded, because the compute behind them is already spent.

This policy sits alongside our Terms of Service and never reduces your statutory rights.

02Who processes your refund

Paddle is our reseller and merchant of record, so Paddle holds the payment transaction and issues refunds. You can request a refund either way:

  • email us at info@evolvixos.com and we will authorise it with Paddle; or
  • contact Paddle directly using the order reference in your receipt email.

Coming to us first is usually faster, and it lets us fix the underlying problem if there is one.

03Your 14-day right of withdrawal (EU and UK consumers)

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days without giving a reason.

One important exception to understand. For digital services that begin immediately, the law lets you consent to starting straight away and acknowledge that you lose the withdrawal right once the service is fully performed. At checkout you may be asked to give that consent so you can use the Service without waiting 14 days.

Where you have given it, we do not treat the withdrawal right as lost outright. Instead we refund the portion you have not used, and deduct only the value of what you actually consumed — which is the fair outcome the law is aiming at.

To withdraw, email info@evolvixos.com within 14 days of purchase with your account email and order reference. A clear statement is enough; no particular form is required.

04Subscription plans

SituationWhat you get
Within 14 days of your first paid subscription Full refund, less the value of Credits consumed beyond the free allowance
Monthly renewal you did not intend Full refund if requested within 7 days of the charge and consumption in the new period was minimal
Annual plan, after 14 days Pro-rata refund of complete unused months, at our discretion
Mid-period cancellation No automatic refund — your plan stays active to the end of the paid period
We materially reduce or discontinue a feature you rely on Pro-rata refund of the unused paid period
Verified outage or defect that prevented substantial use Pro-rata credit or refund for the affected period

Cancelling and refunding are different actions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; a refund returns money already charged. If you want both, say so.

05Credit packs

Purchased Credits are one-time purchases of consumable capacity.

  • Unused Credits — refundable in full within 14 days of purchase.
  • Partly used packs — within 14 days we refund the unused proportion, rounded in your favour to the nearest whole euro.
  • Consumed Credits — not refundable. The compute and third-party model calls behind them have already been paid for and cannot be reversed.
  • After 14 days — purchased Credits do not expire while your Account is active, so they stay available to use rather than being refunded.

If Credits were consumed by a defect on our side, a runaway retry loop in the Service, or unauthorised access to your Account that you reported promptly, tell us — we will restore the Credits rather than treat them as consumed.

06When we may decline a refund

We may refuse a refund where:

  • the request falls outside the windows above and no statutory right applies;
  • the Account breached the acceptable use rules in our Terms of Service;
  • there is evidence of refund abuse, such as repeatedly buying, consuming, and reclaiming the same product, or serial free-trial and refund cycling;
  • Credits were consumed in substantial volume before the request, other than in the fault cases described in section 5;
  • the purchase was made by a business rather than a consumer, and no contractual right to refund applies.

If we decline, we will tell you why and point to the specific ground. You can ask us to reconsider with additional context, and you retain any statutory rights and the ability to escalate to Paddle.

07How to request a refund

Email info@evolvixos.com with:

  • the email address on your Account;
  • the order or receipt reference from Paddle;
  • what you bought and roughly when;
  • the reason — brief is fine, and required only outside the statutory withdrawal window.

Timescales

  • We acknowledge requests within 2 business days.
  • We decide within 5 business days, sooner for clear-cut statutory withdrawals.
  • Approved refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method, typically arriving within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account.

08Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks are slow for you, costly for us, and usually resolvable in a single email.

While a chargeback is open on your Account we may suspend paid features until it is resolved. If a chargeback is decided in our favour and the Account shows a pattern of abuse, we may decline future service.

We would rather fix the problem. Start with info@evolvixos.com.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes apply to purchases made after the new effective date and never retroactively reduce rights attached to a purchase you have already made. Material changes will be announced at least 30 days in advance.

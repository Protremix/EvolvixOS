What is EvolvixOS

One platform. Every AI engine. Zero lock-in.

EvolvixOS is an open-source AI engineering platform that brings together the best open-source AI models under a single, intelligent interface. It automatically selects the right engine for each task.

EvolvixOS is not a wrapper around ChatGPT. It's a full-stack AI platform that runs entirely on your own server. Every model — from Wan2.2 for video generation to Whisper for speech recognition — runs locally, with zero dependency on external APIs for core functionality.

The platform is powered by Mr James, an AI agent with 58 built-in tools that can write code, manage servers, deploy applications, and orchestrate other AI models — all through natural language commands.

OmniRoute gateway adds access to 340+ model providers and 1200+ models when you need premium AI capabilities, while the local Ollama engine handles everyday tasks with zero cost.

ComfyUI serves as the visual engine for image and video generation, supporting all major open-source models including FLUX, Stable Diffusion XL, Wan2.1/2.2, HunyuanVideo, and 30+ more.

How it works

From idea to result in 4 steps

No technical knowledge needed. Just describe what you want.

1
Describe your goal
Type what you want in plain English. "Make a video of a sunset" or "Build a REST API".
2
Auto-engine selection
Mr James analyzes your request and picks the best AI model from 281+ options. You see which engine is chosen and why.
3
AI executes
The selected engine generates the output — video, image, code, audio, or analysis. Mr James monitors and self-corrects if needed.
4
Get your result
Download, share, or deploy. Everything is saved and accessible through the Studio dashboard or API.
